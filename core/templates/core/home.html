How it Works

Your complete guide to using the University Bus Management System.

1. Secure Registration

Sign up using your official university email. Students and faculty get personalized access to their respective route permissions.

2. Live Schedules

View real-time departure and arrival times for all shuttles. Filter by route type (Shuttle/Regular) to find your perfect trip.

3. Smart Booking

Reserve your seat instantly through the dashboard. Get immediate confirmation and access to your digital ticket on your mobile.

4. Live GPS Tracking

Never miss your bus. Use the integrated live map to track the exact location of your shuttle as it approaches your stop.

5. Verified Inspection

Travel with peace of mind. Every bus undergoes regular safety inspections by certified inspectors before hitting the road.

6. Support & Feedback

Report issues or provide feedback directly from your student dashboard. Our support team is here to ensure a smooth journey.
